# Contributing

1. Read [POLICY.md](POLICY.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
2. Keep research within assets you own or have explicit written authorization to test.
3. Record the program, approved assets, exclusions, and authorization date in the authorization template.
4. Prefer passive review and minimal, non-destructive validation.
5. Use synthetic test data. Never collect or commit another person's data.
6. Redact secrets and sensitive evidence before writing notes.
7. Document confirmed impact, safe reproduction steps, and practical remediation.
8. Submit sensitive vulnerability details through the program's approved private disclosure channel, not a public issue or pull request.

Contributions that enable unauthorized access, persistence, disruption, credential theft, or data exfiltration are out of scope.
